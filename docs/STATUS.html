What the baselines get wrong

  • B1 lets through agent mistakes that look harmless to the rules: wrong amounts, duplicates, wrong participants, mistyped domains.
  • B2 is "safe" only because it blocks three quarters of legitimate actions.
  • B3 asks the user on 74% of cases and makes 30 escalations, none of them correct. In 6 of its 8 unsafe allows the user said "No" and it allowed the action anyway.

Risks to watch

What could sink the results, and what to do about it.

GPU budget for 8B training

High

Measured at 111–143 s per step. 2 epochs is 12.5–15 h per model, so all 8 at 2 epochs would take about 100 GPU-hours.

1 epoch; B4 and B5 first (one ~7.5 h session); ablations across team accounts; try Unsloth.

No ESCALATE cases in the test set

High

The 160 executable cases contain 0 ESCALATE, so B4 vs B5 on escalation can't be measured yet.

The ~84 decision-only cases must supply 30+ ESCALATE.

Results are on provisional labels

High

All current numbers use Claude's labels. Some calls are judgement calls a human might label differently (e.g. the mistyped luesparrowtech.com domain).

Freeze the human gold set, then re-run NB02 and NB04. Don't quote the current numbers.

B1 uses oracle provenance

Medium

B1 blocks injections partly because its provenance tags come from the known injected text. That's an upper bound, not a realistic runtime result.

Report it as such, next to the inferred-provenance swap in NB04.

Labelling is behind schedule

Medium

The week-2 checkpoint is 1 of 4 met, and every reportable result depends on the gold labels.

The batch is ready; freeze the guide this week and split labelling across all four members.

Weak B0 baseline

Medium

34.5% task success is low, and 8.3% attack success leaves little room to show security gains end to end, except in banking.

The replay fallback is already built. State the bound in the report.

Handbooks are short

Low

About 2 pages each against the 5–8 pages promised. Fewer clauses also means the Recall@k results (0.52 @3, 0.63 @5) say less.

Extend before freezing, or note the change in the report.
