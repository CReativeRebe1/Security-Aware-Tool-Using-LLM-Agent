Risks to watch

What could sink the results, and what to do about it.

Too few ESCALATE cases

High

The rules gate gives only 9 ESCALATEs out of 1,390 executable cases, but the test set needs ≥30 per class.

Most ESCALATE cases must come from the ~84 decision-only test cases.

GPU budget for 8B training

High

Eight Qwen3-8B adapters on a T4, and none has run yet. The proposal budgets 6–10 h for B4/B5 alone.

Run the NB03 trial now. Train B4/B5 first; ablations only if time allows.

Labelling is behind schedule

Medium

The week-2 checkpoint is 1 of 4 met, and every result depends on the gold labels.

Freeze the guide this week and split labelling across all four members.

Weak B0 baseline

Medium

34.5% task success is low, and 8.3% attack success leaves little room to show security gains end to end, except in banking.

The replay fallback is already built. State the bound in the report.

Branches have split

Medium

Two parallel implementations (sagate and secgate); main has only a README.

Agree on one branch before labelling.

Handbooks are short

Low

About 2 pages each against the 5–8 pages promised. Fewer clauses also means the Recall@k results say less.

Extend before freezing, or note the change in the report.
